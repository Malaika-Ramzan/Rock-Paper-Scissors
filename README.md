# Rock Paper Scissors

### A Fun & Interactive Game — Play Against The Computer.

Rock Paper Scissors is a simple, beautiful and interactive game, built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors and play against the computer. Your Wins, Losses and Draws are counted live, so you can see your score in real time.

---

## Live Demo

> Open `index.html` in any modern browser to play the game.
> No installation or setup is required.

---

## Features

- Play Against The Computer
- Live Score Tracking — Wins, Losses & Draws
- Instant Result Messages
- Clean, Modern & Dark Design
- Fully Responsive — Mobile, Laptop & PC
- Smooth Animations & Hover Effects
- Reset Game Button
- Fast Loading & Lightweight Code

---

## How To Play

1. Choose your weapon — Rock, Paper or Scissors.
2. The computer will choose its weapon at the same time.
3. The result will appear instantly.

### Game Rules

- Rock beats Scissors
- Scissors beats Paper
- Paper beats Rock
- Same choice = Draw
